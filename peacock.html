Smart Peacock Protection

AgroSense uses a mobile rover carrying an ESP32-CAM to capture images around the farm. The system can analyze the captured image and identify a peacock, after which the configured deterrent system can respond.

3D Printed Rover

The rover moves around the agricultural area while carrying the camera and electronics.

📷

ESP32-CAM

The ESP32-CAM mounted on the rover captures photographs of the surroundings when monitoring is required.

📡

PIR Sensor

A PIR sensor can act as a motion trigger, helping initiate image capture when movement is detected.

🧠

AI Recognition

The captured image can be analyzed by a trained AI model to determine whether the detected subject is a peacock.

📢

Infrasonic Deterrent

An infrasonic emitter is represented as one of the configured non-contact deterrent outputs.

💦

Smart Sprinkler

A controller can activate a relay connected to the sprinkler when the required condition is detected.

🌙

Night Deterrent

The project concept includes an IoT-controlled night-time laser deterrent as another system output.

🔄

Automatic Decision

The system can choose the configured response based on detection and operating conditions.
